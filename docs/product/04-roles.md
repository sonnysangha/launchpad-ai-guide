The signed-in user owns the local sample workspace and its saved project. Account ownership determines who can access that data. The People page lists only the current signed-in account, labelled You and Owner.

People also has a permission inspector with Owner, Contributor, and Reviewer buttons. Selecting one shows its conceptual responsibilities; it does not add a member or configure access. An Owner is responsible for the workspace and final setup confirmation. A Contributor would produce the work. A Reviewer would check the work before handoff.

Contributor and Reviewer are educational descriptions in this demonstration. They are not active shared accounts or configurable permissions. There is no member management, invitation delivery, or real client access. Explaining a role never adds a person to the workspace. Do not enter real names, email addresses, or customer details. Another signed-in user gets their own isolated workspace data.
