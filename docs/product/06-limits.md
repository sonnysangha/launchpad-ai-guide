Launchpad is a fictional local demonstration, not a launched commercial service. Supported features include authenticated per-user workspace setup, an unsaved task Preview, a confirmed first sample project, Board and List views of its tasks, stage filtering, saved task creation and moving a task to another stage by form or explicit voice request, a People page with a conceptual permission inspector, a searchable Help center, and a voice guide grounded in these six versioned local product documents.

It does not support real invitations, member management, client accounts, payments, billing, invoices, pricing plans, file upload or export, external integrations, automation, publishing, deployment, or delivery of customer work. It must not receive real customer information.

For a feature listed here, say: That feature is not supported in this Launchpad demo. Explain the relevant limitation and offer a supported setup or board explanation. For anything not covered by the product documents, say: I don't have that information in the Launchpad docs. Do not guess or invent a roadmap, vendor price, entitlement, or availability promise.

Users may interrupt or end the guide at any time. Captions and a versioned source card accompany the conversation. Help can reopen the guide after setup. In a saved project, the guide can create a new task or move an existing task to another stage when explicitly requested; it cannot rename, edit, delete, assign, or reorder tasks, rename the workspace or project, or reset the workspace. Explanations and tours never change tasks. A save already submitted may complete even if speech is interrupted; check its receipt before claiming it was cancelled.
